About VoxLegis

VoxLegis is an editorial initiative of the Faculty of Law, The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, established to provide a considered platform for legal research, analysis, and commentary. It brings together research articles, case comments, and scholarly notes contributed by students and faculty, addressing doctrinal, comparative, and contemporary questions in law.

The publication is intended to encourage rigorous legal writing and informed debate within the academic community, and to make that scholarship accessible to a wider readership interested in the law.
